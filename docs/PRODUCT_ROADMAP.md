# Product Roadmap

## Q3 2026
- **Reinforcement Learning Engine**: Upgrade from Prophet/ARIMA to a deep reinforcement learning model (DQN) that continuously learns from market reactions.
- **Custom BI Dashboards**: Allow users to drag-and-drop widgets to build custom analytics reports.

## Q4 2026
- **Amazon Marketplace Integration**: Native integration with Amazon Seller Central API.
- **Multi-Currency Support**: Dynamic pricing per region/currency.

## Q1 2027
- **B2B Contract Pricing**: Personalized dynamic pricing tailored to specific B2B customers based on their procurement history.
